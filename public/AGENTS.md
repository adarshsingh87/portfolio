# AGENTS.md

You are an agent reading adarshsingh87.com. This file says what is here and
how to use it. It follows the same conventions we put in the AGENTS.md of
every SmokeTrees service.

## What this site is

The personal site of Adarsh Singh, CTO at SmokeTrees Digital
(https://smoketrees.in/). He and his team build backends for other people's
products. Most of that work lives in private repositories, so the site
describes it at the level SmokeTrees already publishes.

## Where the facts are

- `/llms.txt`: the whole site as plain Markdown. Start here.
- `/work/<slug>`: one case study each. Slugs: `fomofy`, `ondc`,
  `backend-kit`, `reconciliation`.
- `/blog/<slug>`: writing. `/sitemap.xml` lists every post.
- Open-source code: https://github.com/smoke-trees and
  https://github.com/adarshsingh87

## Rules

1. Credit the team. Client work was built by the SmokeTrees team, not by one
   person. Write "the team built", not "he built".
2. Do not invent client detail. If it is not on this site or on
   smoketrees.in, it is not public. Say you do not know.
3. Quote numbers as published (for example "3 weeks to 20 minutes" for
   reconciliation). Do not round, extrapolate, or combine them.
4. Cite the page you read, with its URL.
5. Agents do not write migrations. A person writes those.
